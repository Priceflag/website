About

Pricing, minus the guesswork. See what a price change will probably do before it goes live, then roll it out slowly so a bad call stays small.

Why

We've been there.

We ran our own online stores, and so did a bunch of our friends from high school. Every time we had to pick a price, we basically guessed and hoped. Priceflag is the tool we wished we'd had.

What we're building

Three things, done well.

  1. A forecast first.Pick some products and a new price. You get a range of what could happen to orders and profit, plus your breakeven, from your own order history. Not one magic number.
  2. A slow rollout.The new price goes live on a few products, then more, then all of them.
  3. A guardrail that asks you.If orders drop below the forecast, we pause the rollout, let you know, and suggest what to do next. You make the call.
How we build

A few rules we stick to.

  • Same price for everyone.Two shoppers, same product, same day, same price. Always.
  • Ranges over promises.A single number hides how wrong it could be. We show you the spread so you can plan for it.
  • You're in charge.We pause and suggest. Nothing big happens to your store without you saying so.
