About

Pricing, minus the guesswork.

We ran our own online stores, and so did a bunch of our friends from high school. Every time we had to pick a price, we basically guessed and hoped. Priceflag is the tool we wished we'd had: see what a price change will probably do before it goes live, then roll it out slowly so a bad call stays small.

What we're building

Three things, done well.

A forecast first

Pick some products and a new price. You get a range of what could happen to orders and profit, plus your breakeven, worked out from your own order history. Not one magic number.

A slow rollout

The new price goes live on a few products, then more, then all of them. Every shopper sees the same price the whole time.

A guardrail that asks you

If orders drop below the forecast, we pause the rollout, let you know, and suggest what to do next. You make the call.

How we build

A few rules we stick to.

  • Same price for everyone

    Two shoppers, same product, same day, same price. Always.

  • Ranges over promises

    A single number hides how wrong it could be. We show you the spread so you can plan for it.

  • You're in charge

    We pause and suggest. Nothing big happens to your store without you saying so.
