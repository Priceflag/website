Questions worth asking

Short answers about how Priceflag forecasts, rolls out and undoes a price change.

How does the forecast work, and how much data do I need?

Before anything goes live, Priceflag fits your own order history, up to the last 180 days, and shows a range for the change in units and profit, never a single number. It also gives you a breakeven in plain words, for example: you can sell up to 14% fewer units and still make the same profit, and the forecast expects about 9%. Higher-volume products give tighter ranges. If a product has too few orders for a trustworthy forecast, we say so and take smaller steps instead of inventing confidence.

How is the rollout staged, and will shoppers see different prices?

No. Rollouts add products, not visitors. A new price goes live on 25% of the products you selected, then 50%, then all of them, with each step held for a few days before the next begins. At every moment every shopper sees the same price for the same product, so there is no price discrimination and no split testing on your customers. Prices are written to your Shopify catalog like a manual edit in the admin, so your storefront and structured data stay in sync.

How is this different from an A/B testing app?

A/B apps split your traffic, wait, and hand you a chart to interpret. Priceflag forecasts first, rolls out gradually to a slice of your catalog, then compares real daily orders against the range it expected. It keeps checking after the rollout, and every change is journaled so it can be undone. The goal is not an experiment result. It is a price that is still working next quarter.

What happens if a price change hurts sales?

Priceflag checks your orders every day against the expected range. One bad day is noted. Two in a row trips your guardrail: in the beta, that pauses the rollout and emails you, and nothing else changes until you decide. Every change sits in your price journal next to the price it replaced, so Undo puts all of them back with one click and then reads Shopify back to confirm the catalog matches. You also get an audit trail of what changed, what triggered it and when.

Does it change prices without asking me?

No. Priceflag only touches the products you pick, and it proposes each change for you to approve before anything is applied, including before a rollout moves to its next step.

What happens to my prices if I uninstall?

Uninstalling removes our write access, so whatever price is live at that moment stays live. We do not roll anything back on the way out, because a silent rewrite of your catalog would be one more price change you never asked for. If you want the originals back, run Undo from your price journal before you uninstall, or ask us for your price history export and restore them yourself.
