Questions worth asking

Will shoppers see different prices?

No. Every shopper sees the same price at every moment. A rollout adds products, not visitors: 25% of your selection, then 50%, then all.

Do you change prices automatically?

Not without your approval. You approve each change and each rollout step. In the beta, a tripped guardrail pauses the rollout and emails you.

What data do you read?

Products, prices, inventory, cost of goods and up to 180 days of orders from Shopify. No customer names, addresses, emails or phones. See our privacy policy.

How accurate is the forecast?

It gives a range with a breakeven, and says when a product has too few orders. In simulation, 93% of ranges contained the outcome. See how we measure.

What does it cost?

The price review is free. App pricing isn't published yet; we share it before you install.

How long does setup take?

Connecting Shopify is free and takes about 5 minutes. The price review needs no install.

Can I undo everything?

Yes. Every change is journaled with the price it replaced, and Undo restores them. Uninstalling rolls nothing back, so run Undo first.

What about subscriptions?

Products outside your selection keep their price. An included subscription product may move with it, depending on your selling plans. Ask us first: kabir@priceflag.org.
