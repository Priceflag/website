Privacy Policy

Effective September 1, 2026

Draft, not in force yet. A lawyer still needs to review this. Questions? Email hello@priceflag.org.

The short version

  • We read the store data a forecast needs through Shopify's API. We don't sell it, and we don't share it for advertising.
  • We don't keep copies of your order history beyond what a forecast needs.
  • We don't send your store data to third-party AI model providers.
  • There are no analytics cookies on our site today.
  • What we have to keep to run the service is deleted within 30 days of uninstall or a deletion request.

The rest of this page is the detail.

Who we are and what this covers

Priceflag is a pricing tool for Shopify merchants. It forecasts what a price change will probably do, then rolls the new price out to a growing share of the products you picked, in stages over time. Every shopper sees the same price. We never split traffic and never show different shoppers different prices.

Each day we check orders on the products in a rollout against the forecast range. If orders fall below that range, the rollout pauses (no more products get the new price), we let you know, and we give you a recommendation, like hold, continue, or put the old price back. You decide. Nothing is reverted automatically.

This policy covers the Priceflag web platform at dashboard.priceflag.org, the Priceflag Shopify app once it's listed, and the priceflag.org website. The company behind all of it is Priceflag Syndicate, Inc., a Delaware corporation. You can reach us at hello@priceflag.org.

"You" means the merchant, or whoever uses Priceflag for a merchant. "Your customers" means the shoppers who buy from your store. Priceflag is a business tool. We don't build shopper profiles, we don't advertise to your customers, and we don't sell data to anyone.

Our two roles. For your own account data (your name, work email, the store you connect, billing details, and how you use the app), we're the controller under the GDPR and a business under the CCPA. For the data inside your store (products, prices, inventory, orders, and the limited personal data in order records), you're the controller and we're the processor. We handle that data only on your documented instructions. Shopify is a separate party with its own agreements with you, and nothing here changes those.

What we collect

  • Account info. Your name, work email, the identifier we use to sign you in, the Shopify store domains you connect, and the role each teammate has in your workspace.
  • Billing records. We don't bill through Shopify yet. Any fees during a pilot are agreed with you in writing. Once the app is listed in the Shopify App Store, billing may run through Shopify, and Shopify (not us) would handle your card.
  • Store data from Shopify. Products and variants, titles, SKUs, options, inventory levels, cost of goods where you've recorded it, current and past prices, orders and line items, and store settings.
  • Usage data. The experiments you set up, and every rollout, pause and price change we make. Also which screens you open and which features you use.
  • Technical data. IP address, browser and device type, rough location based on IP, and error traces.
  • Support messages. Anything you send us by email or in-app chat.
  • Price review requests. The email and store URL you give us when you sign up for a free price review or email us.
  • Shopper IP addresses. Our pricing model may use them. See below.

Shopify calls order data protected customer data. It treats name, address, email and phone as an extra tier, protected customer fields, which an app has to request one by one and justify. The exact permissions Priceflag asks for show up on Shopify's install screen before you approve anything. That screen is the official record of what we can see.

Shopper IP addresses

Our pricing model may use shopper IP addresses. We handle them securely: encrypted in transit and at rest, with access restricted. We use them only to understand demand (for example, approximate region). We never use them to show different shoppers different prices, and we never sell them.

Free price reviews and email

There's no waitlist. You can email hello@priceflag.org or sign up for a free price review. We use the email and store URL you give us only to reply to you and do the review. We don't analyze a store's public data unless the merchant asks us to and gives permission.

How we use your info

  • To run the service: forecasts, staged rollouts, breakeven and margin math, and guardrail checks.
  • To write price changes to your store, only for the products and experiments you've enrolled.
  • To tell you when a rollout pauses because orders fell below the forecast range (with our recommendation), and when a rollout ends.
  • To bill you for any fees we've agreed, keep account records, and prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To fix bugs, keep the service secure and up, and make forecasts better for your store.
  • To answer support requests and send service notices. Marketing email only goes to people who asked for it, and every one has an unsubscribe link.

We don't use one merchant's store data to make forecasts for another. We don't train shared models on your data unless you separately and explicitly opt in. We don't send your store data to third-party AI model providers. Protected customer data is used only to deliver the features you turned on. Shopify's API terms forbid anything else, and so does this policy.

Legal bases (GDPR and UK GDPR)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies to data we control, we rely on:

  • Contract for accounts, experiments, billing and support.
  • Legitimate interests for security, abuse prevention, debugging and aggregate usage.
  • Consent for marketing email and non-essential cookies.
  • Legal obligation for tax, accounting and lawful requests.

For data inside your store, you pick the legal basis, since you're the controller.

Shopify access and price changes

  • Priceflag connects through Shopify's standard OAuth install. You see the exact permissions before you grant them, and you can revoke them any time from your Shopify admin.
  • We use write access for two things only: setting a price on a product you enrolled in an experiment, and putting the previous price back when you choose to.
  • We don't touch orders, customers, fulfillment, discounts, or any field outside the pricing fields an experiment uses.
  • Every price write is logged with a timestamp, the experiment behind it, and the before and after values. You can see that log in the app.
  • Uninstalling the app cuts our access right away. Whatever price is live at that moment stays live, so get your prices where you want them before you uninstall.

Shopify's privacy webhooks

Shopify requires every app to handle three compliance webhooks. We check the HMAC signature on each one and reject anything that fails.

  • customers/data_request. One of your customers asked you for the data held about them. We send what we hold to you within the 30 days Shopify allows, and you answer your customer.
  • customers/redact. You asked, for a customer, that their data be erased. We delete or permanently redact that customer's records and their orders.
  • shop/redact. Sent 48 hours after you uninstall. We erase the data we hold for that shop.

Separately, Shopify's API License and Terms of Use require an app to delete every copy of a merchant's data within 30 days of uninstall or termination, unless the law requires us to keep something. We treat that as a hard rule.

Keeping data safe

We run the controls Shopify requires of every app that handles protected customer data: encryption in transit and at rest, encrypted backups, set retention periods, separate test and production environments, staff access limited to people who need it, logging of access to protected customer data, strong sign-in on staff accounts, and a written incident response process. There's a plain summary on our Security page.

We don't hold any third-party security certification. We're not SOC 2 audited and not ISO 27001 certified, and we won't claim either until it's true. If that matters for your procurement, ask us where we stand before you install.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects personal data we hold, we'll tell you without undue delay so you can meet your own obligations as controller. We'll also notify regulators where the law requires it. Under the GDPR, that's within 72 hours of becoming aware of a notifiable breach.

Who we work with

We use a small number of vendors to run Priceflag. Each one that processes personal data for us is bound by contract to use it only on our instructions and only to provide their service to us, on terms that meet Article 28 of the GDPR. This is every vendor that touches data today:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) hosts the Priceflag product and stores its data.
  • Vercel hosts the priceflag.org website.
  • Shopify is the platform your store runs on and where the data we read comes from. Once the app is listed, Shopify may also bill app subscriptions.
  • Stripe was used to form the company and may process payments in the future. Stripe doesn't get any store data.
  • Our email provider handles mail to and from hello@priceflag.org.

We don't use any analytics or error-monitoring vendors today. We don't send your store data to third-party AI model providers. We'll update this list before adding any vendor that touches store data.

Beyond that, we only share data when the law makes us, to establish or defend legal claims, to protect the rights and safety of our users or the public, or in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets. In that last case, we'll tell you before your data falls under a different policy. We don't sell personal information, and we don't share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as the CCPA defines those terms. We never have.

How long we keep things

  • Store, product and order data. We read what a forecast needs through Shopify's API. We don't keep copies of order history beyond what a forecast needs, and we don't sell it or share it for advertising.
  • What we have to keep to run the service. Your shop address, access token, staged prices and rollout history. This is deleted within 30 days of uninstall or a deletion request.
  • Account records. Kept while your account is open, then deleted after a short wind-down window so you can reinstall if you want.
  • Price-change logs. Kept while you're a customer, exportable any time, and deleted along with your rollout history within 30 days of uninstall or a deletion request.
  • Billing and tax records. Kept as long as tax and company law requires. That requirement wins over a deletion request.
  • Technical logs. Kept for a limited operational period, then deleted or aggregated so they can't identify anyone.

Backups expire on their own schedule, so data removed from live systems can sit in backups for a short time before it's overwritten.

Your rights

If the GDPR or UK GDPR applies to you, you can access the personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, get it in a portable machine-readable format, restrict or object to how we use it, object to direct marketing at any time, and withdraw consent you gave before.

  • A lot of this is self-serve: you can export your data from the app, and uninstalling the Shopify app stops any further collection right away.
  • For anything else, email hello@priceflag.org. We'll reply within one month. For truly complex requests we may take up to two more months, and we'll tell you within the first month if so. We may need to confirm who you are first.
  • If the request comes from one of your customers, it's yours to handle as controller of that data. Forward it and we'll give you what you need, or use Shopify's customers/data_request and customers/redact flows, which reach us automatically.
  • You can complain to the data protection authority where you live or work. Priceflag Syndicate, Inc. is a Delaware corporation based in California, United States.

We won't treat you differently, degrade your service, or charge you more for using any of these rights.

California

The CCPA, as amended by the CPRA, gives California residents the right to know what personal information we've collected and where it came from, to access and delete it, to correct it, to get it in a portable form, and to limit the use of sensitive personal information. Since 2023 these rights cover business-to-business contexts too, so they apply to you as a merchant.

The categories we collect are identifiers, commercial information and internet activity. We don't sell or share personal information, so there's nothing to opt out of and no "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link. If that ever changes, we'll say so here first. For store data we act as a service provider, and we never keep, use or disclose it for our own purposes.

To make a request, email hello@priceflag.org. We'll confirm we got it within 10 business days and respond within 45 days, which we can extend once.

International transfers

The Priceflag product runs on Amazon Web Services (AWS), and the website is hosted on Vercel. When we move EEA, UK or Swiss personal data to a country without an adequacy decision, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (Decision 2021/914), plus the UK International Data Transfer Addendum where UK data is involved, a transfer risk assessment, and extra technical safeguards. You can ask us for a copy of the clauses we rely on.

Cookies and tracking

The site and the app use a small number of cookies and similar tech. The strictly necessary ones keep you signed in, remember your workspace, and protect against cross-site request forgery. You can't switch those off.

There are no analytics cookies today. If we add analytics later, we'll update this policy first and ask for your consent before setting any analytics cookie, through a consent banner where the law requires it.

We don't use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking pixels, or any cookie that follows you off our own sites. You can block or clear cookies in your browser, but the app won't work properly without the necessary ones.

Kids

Priceflag is a business tool sold to merchants. It's not meant for children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you think a child has given us data, write to us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this policy as the product and the law change. The date at the top always shows the current version. If a change materially affects how we handle your data, we'll tell you by email or in the app before it takes effect, and we'll ask for consent where the law requires it.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or want to make a privacy request? Email hello@priceflag.org. Signed data processing agreements, copies of our Standard Contractual Clauses, and the current sub-processor list are available from Priceflag Syndicate, Inc., a Delaware corporation, at the same address.
