Built for Shopify
See the predicted impact on profit before anything goes live. Roll the change out gradually. We watch your real orders and pause the rollout and alert you if it stops paying.
Forecast it. Roll it out. Undo it.
See what it will do before it goes live.
Pick the products and Priceflag never touches anything else. You get a range and the breakeven in plain words, never a single number.
Try it on a few products first.
The new price goes live on 25% of the products you picked, then 50%, then all of them. Every shopper sees the same price the whole time. Two bad days in a row pauses the rollout and emails you.
Put every price back with one click.
Every change sits in your price journal with the price it replaced. Undo restores all of them and reads Shopify back to confirm.
