How a price change goes live

  1. Connect Shopify

    Free, and it takes about 5 minutes. Connecting changes no prices.

  2. Propose a price

    Pick the products and type the new price. Priceflag touches nothing else.

  3. See the forecast and the breakeven

    The forecast is a range built from your own order history, shown next to your breakeven.

  4. Roll out by SKU cohort, with guardrails set before launch

    The price goes live on 25% of the selected SKUs, then 50%, then all. Every shopper sees the same price.

Example numbers: a $24.00 product with a $9.60 unit cost, moving to $27.00 across 12 SKUs.
