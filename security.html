Security

Effective September 1, 2026

Draft, not in force yet. A lawyer still needs to review this. Questions? Email hello@priceflag.org.

A plain summary of how we look after your data. The Privacy Policy has the full detail.

What we can access in Shopify

We ask Shopify for permission to read products and prices, write prices on the products you select, and read orders. Shopify shows you the exact list before you approve anything, and you can revoke it any time from your Shopify admin.

We use write access only to set a price on a product you enrolled, and to put the previous price back when you choose to. We don't touch orders, customers, fulfillment or discounts. Every price write is logged with a timestamp, the experiment behind it, and the before and after values, and you can see that log in the app.

Where your data lives

The Priceflag product and its data run on Amazon Web Services (AWS). The priceflag.org website is hosted separately on Vercel.

We read the store data a forecast needs through Shopify's API. We don't keep copies of order history beyond what a forecast needs.

Encryption

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and so are our backups. Shopper IP addresses are encrypted and access-restricted, used only to understand demand, and never used to show different shoppers different prices.

Who can see it

Only the people at Priceflag who need it to run the service and support you. Staff accounts use strong sign-in, and access to protected customer data is logged. We keep test and production environments separate.

We don't sell your data or share it for advertising. We don't send your store data to third-party AI model providers.

Certifications

We don't hold any third-party security certification. We're not SOC 2 audited and not ISO 27001 certified, and we won't claim either until it's true. If that matters for your procurement, ask us where we stand before you install.

If something goes wrong

We have a written incident response process. No system is perfectly secure, so if a breach affects personal data we hold, we'll tell you without undue delay, and notify regulators where the law requires it.

Deletion

What we have to keep to run the service (shop address, access token, staged prices and rollout history) is deleted within 30 days of uninstall or a deletion request. To ask, email hello@priceflag.org.

Questions

Security questions, or something that looks off? Email hello@priceflag.org.
