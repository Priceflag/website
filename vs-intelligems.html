Priceflag vs Intelligems

Both tools help a Shopify store find a better price. They go about it in different ways: Priceflag changes the price for everyone, one group of products at a time, while price A/B testing shows different shoppers different prices at the same time.

How Priceflag changes a price

Before anything goes live, Priceflag shows a forecast range for orders and profit, and a breakeven: how many orders you could lose and still make the same profit. How we measure explains both.

Then the new price rolls out by product and over time, never by visitor. Every shopper sees the same price.

  1. 25%of the products you picked
  2. 50%of the products you picked
  3. Allof the products you picked

At each stage, real daily orders are checked against the forecast. In the beta, a tripped guardrail pauses the rollout and emails you. It never rolls a price back by itself; you decide what happens next. Every change is journaled with the price it replaced, so it can be undone.

How price A/B testing works

A price A/B test splits visitors into groups, and each group sees a different price for the same product at the same time. Comparing the groups shows which price did better. To tell a real difference from noise, a test usually needs weeks of split traffic.

Split testing is good at something a rollout cannot do: because both prices run side by side, a seasonal swing or a big promotion hits both groups alike, so the comparison is clean. It also works for more than prices, such as shipping offers or page content.

What Intelligems says about its product

Everything below is taken from Intelligems’ own public pages, linked so you can check it. Checked on Oct 13, 2026.

  • It describes itself as a tool to A/B test prices and offers for ecommerce, testing and personalizing pricing, content and offers, and it works with Shopify (intelligems.io).
  • It lets you test several price points side by side on a product or collection, and show different prices to different customer segments (Intelligems price testing page).
  • It reports profit per visitor for each variant, counting cost of goods, discounts and shipping, and you then pick the winner and roll it out (price testing page, intelligems.io).
  • Its documentation lists tests and personalization for content, prices, discounts, shipping rates, checkout and post-purchase upsells, measured on conversion rate, average order value, revenue per visitor and gross profit per visitor (Intelligems docs).

Which fits your store

If you have the traffic to wait for a split test and are comfortable showing shoppers different prices, a split test gives you a clean side-by-side comparison. If you want every shopper to see one price, or you do not have weeks of traffic per product, Priceflag gives you a forecast and a breakeven first and limits how much of your catalog a wrong price can reach.
